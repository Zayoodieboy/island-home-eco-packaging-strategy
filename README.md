# island-home-eco-packaging-strategy
Marketing Strategy Plan (MSP) for Island Home featuring SWOT/TOWS analysis, Porter's 5 Forces, market positioning, and a Visily wireframe model for plastic-free, home-compostable banana leaf packaging.

# Assignment 2 – MSP

## Introduction
This report aims for each group of marketing students to develop a formal marketing plan to resolve a real-world marketing problem.

This report will be divided into several parts:
* The situation analysis is vital to the marketing planning process as it provides the back-bone analysis that supports the marketing decision-making process.
* The report will explain the stakeholder analysis that involves identifying relevant factors, like investors, employees, customers, and many more, to understand the client’s behaviour, intentions, agendas, interests, and the influence or resources students have brought on decision-making processes.
* The report will provide two types of statements for the food organization: problem & opportunities statement and the overall positioning statement for the firm, marketing goal, and objectives.
* The paper will identify relevant segmentation variables, segment the market, and develop profiles for each segment.
* The data will propose strategic recommendations corresponding to each marketing mix.

The client in this report is Island Home. Michele Senanayake and Charith Senanayake have worked on this organic, conversation food production sector since 2004. The business operates from a commercial kitchen in Carlton. Their food product is Sri Lankan cuisine prepared with healthy low GI rice. Additionally, all of their ready-made meals are packed with plastic-free home compostable banana leaf wrapping.

## Situation Analysis

### SWOT/TWOS Analysis

#### Internal Analysis

##### Strengths:
* **Agile:** One of the great strengths of Island Home is that they are agile. For example, Vivify (2019) suggested that being agile allows food production teams like Island home to adapt and market changes quickly and, at the same time, it enables the organization to have adjustable project management strategies. Therefore, being agile allows team members to interact and cooperate in multiple ways.
* **New And Innovative Product:** Product innovation in the food industry is important because the operators in the food preparation sector can create new spaces in a seemingly crowded market, emphasised by Ghelber (2019). Innovation in the food industry is usually the combination of social and cultural innovation and that’s what Island Home is known for.
* **Local Supplier Chain:** Since Island Home localizes its supply chain, that means it can represent excellent advantages for the environment. Team Thomas (2020) implied that the food production sector contributes to green manufacturing when sourcing locally but, it also helps build consumer confidence. Furthermore, the blog (2020) also mentions that food organizations that source locally leads to easy and faster communication.
* **Healthy Food Quality:** Island Home has good food quality, and their food product is made up of vegan and natural food, which helps maintain healthy body weight. Queensland Health (2019) stated, “when a customer follows a healthy eating pattern could be linked with better stress management, improved sleep quality, and in general better mental wellbeing.

##### Weaknesses:
* **Low capitalization:** Small caps tend to be more voile and riskier investments. Ross (2021) implied that when a firm has low capitalization, that means they have less access to capital and do not have many financial resources.

#### External Analysis

##### Opportunities:
* **Environmentally appropriate and responsive:** Since Island Home is known to be environmentally appropriate and responsive, that means they use specific packaging techniques that would help protect the environment. Being environmentally relevant and responsive plays a significant role in developing prepared meal production and it is known to be ecologically beneficial towards the food industry, suggested by Lockie (2006, p.191).
* **Exotic cuisine with little market saturation:** Usually, when a business has little market saturation, the company faces intense competition in the food industry. Cloutman (2021, p.30) stated that “the competition in the prepared meal production industry is high and has risen over the past several years in Australia.” Moreover, Island Home is a Sri Lakin cuisine, meaning this could make customers widen their boundaries. Food VacBags (2020) suggested that it could lead customers to eat fresher, introduce them to different cultures, and add more nutrients to their diet.
* **Plastic free:** Island Home uses plastic-free packaging, as in they use banana leaves as their food packaging. One of the main benefits of using environmentally friendly packaging is that it reduces the amount of waste sent to landfills, pointed by Thomas (2015). Furthermore, the author (2015) indicated that when a food production business switches to non-plastic packaging for their meal package, the company will save a significant amount of income in the long run.
* **Home Delivered Healthy Meals:** When customers order meals at home from a local business, that means they are encouraging job creation. In addition, home delivery meals demonstrate great creativity and the desire to please their customers and offer quality menus and fresh, first-rate products, as indicated by Beurre et Baguette (2021). Moreover, Robinson, Segal, and Segal (2020) mention that getting home delivery healthy meals could support customers’ immune systems and reduce the risk of illnesses like heart disease, high blood pressure, and diabetes.

##### Threats:
* **Potential for competition to arise by more mature well-known operators:** The threat level in of existing rivalry among competitors is relatively high. Cloutman (2021, p.17) stated that "the competition on private-label ready-made production sector is assumed to place further pressure on the food industry, which the operators in this food sector are struggling against cheaper and increasingly higher quality private-label products from the supermarkets."
* **Pandemic buying is erratic:** When the pandemic happened, the food supply chain needed to adjust their consideration quickly to consumers' panic buying and changes in food purchasing patterns, as implied by Hobbs (2020, p.171). Furthermore, Hobbs (2020, p.172) pointed out that even though the government worldwide increased their social distancing policies, most customers had engaged in stockpiling behaviour due to the restrictions' strict rules and regulations.

The SWOT analysis table indicated that Island Home has tremendous strengths and opportunities to make its food sector unique to its existing competitors. In strengths, the food business is known to produce and bring healthy food quality. Nowadays, most customers tend to eat healthy food because they don't want to risk getting diabetes, high cholesterol, or other negative health outcomes. Furthermore, in opportunities, their business is known to be plastic-free. This shows that the organization cares about the environment and aims to become a sustainable and organic prepared meal production sector in Australia. However, most ready-made meal business uses disposable plastic and non-recyclable containers for their food packaging. While Island Home uses banana wrapping leaves as their primary source of food packaging.

Moreover, the organic and natural food business has a weakness; they face the financial issue of low capitalization. Usually, companies that meet low capitalization tend to have difficulty accessing or maintaining their financial resources. Finally, for threats, Island Home faces some high-level threats of potential existing competitors and how consumers' purchasing behaviour has affected their business due to the pandemic. Nonetheless, the TWOS analysis below indicates strategies for how Island Home could solve its weakness and threats.

## Porter’s 5 Forces

| Element | Threat Level | Explanation |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Threat of New Entrants** | **High** | Adamkasi (2017) stated that “the threat level of entry of new entrants is extremely high in the food production sector” |
| **Threat of Substitute Products or Services** | **High** | Most of the target customers in the food production sector tend to eat fast food meals from a non-organic company, implied by Cloutman (2021, p.31) |
| **Bargaining Power of Buyers** | **High** | If target customers are price conscious and well educated about the product, then the bargaining power is high, The Strategic CFO (2021) |
| **Bargaining Power of Suppliers** | **Low** | Suppose that there significant amount of suppliers in the food production industry. In that case, switching costs are high for suppliers, pointed out by Corporate Financial Institute (2021) |
| **Existing Rivalry Among Competitors** | **Increasing and High** | Cloutman (2021, p.30) stated that “the competition in the prepared meal production industry is high and has risen over the past several years in Australia” |

* **Threats of New Entrants:** the threat level is high. Adamkasi (2017) affirmed that it is cheaper to start a food production business than any other type of business and how individuals can easily acquire their funds compared to starting any other type of business.
* **Threat of Substitute Products or Services:** the threat level is high. Garcia (2014, p.14) indicated that most of the target customers in the organic food preparation industry are constantly eating fast food meals. Statista Research Department (2021) analysed that 43.73% of respondents in Australia say that they eat fast food once per week.
* **Bargaining Power of Buyers:** the threat level is high. Cloutman (2021, p.13) indicated that consumers in this food production sector had become more price-sensitive and how many individual target customers are finding ways to reduce food preparation time and, at the same, look for a better products and services that they can purchase.
* **Bargaining Power of Suppliers:** the threat level is low. Garcia (2014, p.16) suggested that the operators like Island Home tend to get their organic products from sustainable farms or food markets; the reason why they buy their products from there is that it is much cheaper than buying it in supermarkets.
* **Existing Rivalry Among Competitors:** the threat level is relatively high. Cloutman (2021, p.17) stated that "the competition on private-label ready-made production sector is assumed to place further pressure on the food industry, which the operators in this food sector are struggling against cheaper and increasingly higher quality private-label products from the supermarkets."
